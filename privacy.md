# Kunde Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

Kunde helps you learn Kazakh words. It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or contacts.

## Data on your device
Your learning progress and settings are stored only on your device. Your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iOS) may include them; that backup is governed by Google's or Apple's policy. Reminders are scheduled on the device.

## Services we use
- **Google AdMob** shows ads in the free version. It may use your device's advertising identifier and approximate location to serve and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent first; on iOS, the app asks before tracking. You can change ad choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **RevenueCat** processes in-app purchases using an anonymous app user ID and your store receipt. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- **Sentry** receives error reports when the app crashes or fails: device model, operating system, app version, and technical error details. They contain no personal information. See https://sentry.io/privacy/

We do not sell your data.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by it. To remove it from device backups, delete the backup in your phone's settings.

## Children
Kunde is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
